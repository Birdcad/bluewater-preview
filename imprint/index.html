Data protection

The protection of your personal data is a high priority for Bluewater Medical. You can use this website without providing any personal data. Processing of personal data is always carried out in line with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the applicable national data protection regulations.

Server log files

When you access this website, general information such as browser type and version, operating system, referring page, date and time of access and IP address is stored in server log files. This data is used only to deliver the website correctly, to keep our systems secure and, if necessary, to support law enforcement in the event of an attack. No conclusions are drawn about individual visitors.

Contact by email

If you contact us by email, the personal data you transmit is stored for the purpose of handling your request. It is not passed on to third parties.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details above.
