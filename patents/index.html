Patent portfolio

The engineers of Bluewater Medical have been inventing in the medical industry for decades and are named on more than 40 patents. Below is a selection used in market-leading products sold today.

Spine

US 6,183,472 B1

Pedicle Screw and Assembly Aid Therefor

A pedicle screw with features for top-loading a tool that helps insert the spinal rod into the screw, while still allowing top-loading and tightening of the assembly. The instrument became known as the Persuader.

US 6,296,647 B1

Instrument for the Positioning of an Implant in the Human Spine

An instrument for positioning implants in the human spine.

External Fixation

US 9,295,492 B2

Fixation Clamp

A further improved fixation clamp that accepts multiple diameters on each side and can be dismantled without tools for cleaning.

EP 1627608 B1 & US 7,491,008 B2

External Fixation Clamp

A single-use coupling with special hinge and force-transmission features, plus protection against re-use.

US 7,527,626 B2

MRI Safe Clamp and Rods

An MRI-safe fixator with insulated carbon rods that interrupt the electrical loop formed by the fixator, reducing eddy currents and the heating of pins that can harm the patient.

Bone Plating

US 8,142,485 B2

Bone Connection Device

An insert that turns a non-locking compression hole into a locking screw hole — flexible placement of locking screws without threads in every plate hole.

US 7,648,508 B2

Bone Plating Implants, Instrument and Methods

Implant and instrument features that facilitate minimally invasive bone plating. A broad patent disclosing several new MIS plating approaches.

Intramedullary Nailing

US 8,685,034 B2

Distal Targeting Device

An improved instrument system for targeting and inserting distal locking screws, building on the distal-locking technique first described by Hans P. Granhed in 1998.

US 7,147,643 B2

Implantation System and Aiming Device

A system of intramedullary nails and instruments that facilitates distal targeting and insertion of distal locking screws.

US 7,763,022 B2

Locking Nail for Fractures of the Proximal Femur

A special geometry around the lag-screw opening reduces local stress, allowing a smaller outer diameter at equal fatigue strength.
