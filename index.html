GLW Medical

Artemis PFN Hip Fracture Nailing System

An intramedullary nailing platform for intertrochanteric and subtrochanteric fractures, combining a titanium nail with carbon-fibre-reinforced PEEK.

  • Titanium alloy nail, partially over-molded with CF-PEEK
  • For stable and unstable intertrochanteric and subtrochanteric fractures
  • Lag screw with optional anti-rotation locking pin

GLW Medical

Creed Cannulated Compression Screws

Headed and headless cannulated compression screws with radiotranslucent “Ortholucent” technology for better intra-operative visualization.

  • Headed and headless screws, Ø2.5 / 4.3 / 5.6 / 7.4 mm
  • Self-drilling and self-tapping, cannulated for guidewire placement
  • Radiotranslucent “Ortholucent” material for better imaging

A motion-preserving, non-fusion implant for the lumbar spine, designed to reduce chronic pain from degenerative disease.

Hansson DCN — Dynamic Cephalomedullary Nail

A cephalomedullary nail system for trochanteric fractures of the proximal femur, with dynamic head fixation.
