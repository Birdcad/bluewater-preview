About Bluewater Medical

An independent medical device development and consulting practice for traumatology, orthopaedics and spine surgery — founded and led by Christian Lutz.

Christian Lutz

Christian works as an independent consultant and development partner through Bluewater Medical, supporting manufacturers, surgeons and inventors in realizing their product ideas. He draws on a network of designers, vendors and key opinion leaders built over around 30 years in medical device product development.

Alongside his consultancy work, he is a part-time Research Associate at the Centre for Clinical Research of the BG Klinikum Hamburg.

Work principles

How we think

Seek the opportunity in adversity

We focus on the fields where others struggle — the design of the device, the complexity of the R&D process, or cost pressure on the product. Master those, and a competitive advantage follows.

“If we knew what we were doing it wouldn’t be called research and development.”— Albert Einstein

Understand the user needs

Rather than just developing the suggested solution, we work to understand what drives the customer and how they measure success. This often leads to surprising, better innovations.

“If I had asked my customers what they wanted, they would have said a faster horse.”— Henry Ford

Interdisciplinary execution

Some projects are like sailboats: they have a hull speed set by design. If you sail slower, something is wrong. We analyse the interfaces between functions so projects run without losing time to market.

“If you want to build a ship, teach them to long for the endless immensity of the sea.”— Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

Achieve more with less

Keep the design simple but not simplistic, and optimise manufacturing cost. Over a product’s lifecycle, manufacturing cost affects return on investment more than rarely-used features.

“The ingenuity of a design lies within its simplicity. Everybody can do complicated.”— Sergei Korolev
